Charles Schwab's trading platform experienced a brief order-routing outage this morning, delaying some retail equity orders by several minutes. A Schwab spokesperson said the issue was resolved by 10:15 AM ET and that no customer accounts were affected. Alpaca's systems were not impacted by the outage. Separately, Microsoft (MSFT) shares climbed sharply after a broker upgraded the stock to Overweight, citing accelerating Azure growth.
